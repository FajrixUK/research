# Contributing to Fajrix Research

[Back to Fajrix Research](README.md)

We welcome specific corrections, relevant primary sources and thoughtful questions about the research direction described here.

## Corrections and source suggestions

Open an issue in this repository and include:

- The page, passage or research question you are referring to.
- The correction, source or question you would like us to consider.
- A source link and relevant date or location where available.
- A short explanation of how it changes or strengthens the reasoning.

Use public, shareable information in issues. For an enquiry that needs a private conversation, contact [info@fajrix.com](mailto:info@fajrix.com).

## Proposing a change

Small documentation corrections can be proposed through a pull request. Explain the reason for the change and include supporting sources where relevant. For a substantial research contribution, open an issue to discuss the scope before preparing a large submission.

We will need to establish provenance and appropriate reuse terms before incorporating externally contributed code, data or other substantive materials.

## Research collaboration

For collaboration enquiries, email [info@fajrix.com](mailto:info@fajrix.com) with the research question, your relevant experience and the contribution you have in mind.

Please keep discussion professional, evidence-focused and respectful of differing interpretations. Submission does not imply acceptance or a commitment to a response date.
