# Study companion — publication template

[Back to Fajrix Research](../README.md) · [Research approach](../RESEARCH-APPROACH.md) · [Research register](../RESEARCH-REGISTER.md)

> **Template only — not a published study or research result.** Copy this document into a study-specific location and replace every instruction and placeholder before publication. Do not link a study as published until its evidence is accessible and reviewed.

## Publication identity

- **Study identifier:** Assign an identifier in the public register.
- **Title:** Insert a precise, descriptive title.
- **Status:** Proposed / In progress / Under review / Published / Superseded / Withdrawn.
- **Release version and date:** Identify the version and date when applicable.
- **Evidence cutoff:** Date through which sources or measurements were considered.
- **Authors and contributions:** Identify contributors and their roles.
- **Review performed:** Record checks actually completed and whether a genuinely independent review occurred.
- **Rights and reuse:** Identify the applicable licence or permissions for each released artefact.

## Research question and scope

State the decision or problem being examined, the intended audience, precise scope, relevant jurisdiction, exclusions and whether the work is exploratory, evaluative or conceptual.

## Hypothesis or analytical framework

Describe any question or hypothesis fixed before data collection. Separate pre-specified analysis from additional exploratory findings.

## Source register and data provenance

Identify original sources, owners, dates, versions, units, access conditions and permissions. Explain any synthetic data generation, de-identification, sampling or exclusions.

## Method and evaluation protocol

Describe the workflow, environment, inputs, controls, baseline, instruments, configuration, success and failure conditions, metrics, sample size and repetition strategy. Record relevant version identifiers without exposing sensitive operational details.

## Results and supporting artefacts

Present reproducible observations and calculations with their units and evidence references. Link scripts, datasets, test cases, plots or independent verification instructions where permitted. Do not substitute a demonstration for measured evidence.

## Failure cases, uncertainty and limitations

Include negative and inconclusive results, error severity, sensitivity to assumptions, comparison limitations, possible confounders and limits of generalisation.

## Interpretation and decision relevance

Explain which conclusions follow from the findings and which do not. Keep recommendations, forecasts and author judgement separate from observed results.

## Security, privacy and publication review

Record the checks completed for rights, confidentiality, sensitive configuration, personal information, financial claims, accessibility, factual support and source links. State any limitations on disclosure.

## Reproduction and access

Identify the exact released version, dependencies, commands or procedures, expected outputs, access restrictions and whether an independent party can meaningfully reproduce the claim.

## Revision and correction history

Record dates, changes, reasons and any effect on conclusions. Retain links to superseded material where practical.
