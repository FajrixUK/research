## Possible future study briefs

The following are **illustrative candidates only**. They have not been registered as active studies, scheduled or assigned study identifiers.

- **Transaction acknowledgement under failure:** Compare committed, uncommitted and unacknowledged financial operations using synthetic transactions, explicit failure injection and fresh-connection verification.
- **Accounting assistance versus deterministic controls:** Specify a reviewable synthetic-document evaluation covering source traceability, decision errors, cost of verification and limitations of model advice.
- **National AI capability comparison:** Define measurable and non-equivalent indicators of investment, adoption, infrastructure and value capture before drawing country-level conclusions.

Candidates require a bounded protocol, permission to publish evidence and an approved scope before their status changes.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed | A specific research question and candidate scope are documented; no execution is implied |
| In progress | Work has begun and the recorded scope and method can be described |
| Under review | A draft and its supporting evidence are undergoing recorded checks |
| Published | A dated research companion and its disclosed evidence are publicly accessible |
| Superseded | A newer publication replaces all or part of an earlier one, with a clear link |
| Withdrawn | A released conclusion or artefact is no longer supported, with a visible explanation |

**Agenda direction** is a programme label, not a study status. A future published study will receive an identifier, title, status, evidence cutoff, version, companion link, limitations and correction history.

## Publication and change control

New entries will link to a completed [study companion](templates/STUDY-COMPANION.md) and will identify the review actually carried out. We will not label results independently verified, peer reviewed or production validated without explicit supporting evidence.

Changes to material findings, corrections and withdrawals should remain visible. A revision to this register cannot itself substitute for publication of the underlying work.
